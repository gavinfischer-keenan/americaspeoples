    Landscape Modification and Subsistence Strategy

    Environmental and structural investigations reveal that the initial colonists established sophisticated adaptations to ensure long-term survival on the sandy islet Burley2016Burley2018. To overcome the challenges of a porous coralline substrate, settlers excavated an artificial cultivation pit shortly after landfall to intercept the Ghyben-Herzberg freshwater lens Burley2018. Sedimentary microfossil analyses within the organic mulch matrix identify introduced Pacific cultigens, including giant swamp taro (Cyrtosperma merkusii), true taro (Colocasia esculenta), banana (Musa sp.), and coconut (Cocos nucifera) Burley2018. Numerous discarded Lapita potsherds were mixed into the silty-sand pit matrix, likely to promote moisture retention and release mineral nutrients as they weathered Burley2018.
