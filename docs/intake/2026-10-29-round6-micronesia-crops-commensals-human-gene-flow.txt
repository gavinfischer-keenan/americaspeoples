    Human Gene Flow and Sociopolitical Networks

    Exchange spheres acted as conduits for human genes, structuring modern and ancient genomic variation Lum2000Carson2013. Rather than remaining isolated after initial settlement, communities were reshaped by recurring demographic inputs from Asia, Melanesia, and Polynesia Luu2022Lum2000Carson2013. High volcanic islands and central coral atolls were formally linked by institutionalized systems—such as the sawei tribute network centered on Yap—which facilitated long-distance mobility, ceremonial exchange of shell valuables, and biological intermarriage Allen2025
    Fitzpatrick2008Intoh2017
    .
        Western Micronesians in the Marianas initially retained pure East Asian ancestry, remaining distinct from early Lapita groups Larson2007
        Lum2000Intoh2017.
        Central-eastern island populations absorbed significant Papuan genetic influxes associated with mainland New Guinea-related source groups Lum2000Intoh2017
        .
        Ancient skeletal remains from Fais preserve the Polynesian motif (B4a1a1a) and its ancestral haplotype, confirming gene flow from eastern corridors Intoh2017
        .
        High maternal mtDNA differentiation between communities reveals strict matrilocal social organization, where female lineages remained rooted locally while voyaging sustained male-mediated gene flow and trade Carson2013.
