    Pacific colonization research tracks the chronological sequencing and maritime dispersals that separated initial western expansions from later eastern voyaging.

    The research arc reflects an initial paradigm of broad, multi-millennium occupation sequences that was later radically revised by chronometric hygiene protocols into a concise, rapid East Polynesian expansion.

    Voyaging Corridors and Line Island Stepping Stones

    The northern outliers of Kiribati occupied a unique strategic position during the later pulse of Polynesian voyaging networks.

    This chronological divergence emphasizes that the Gilbert Islands of Kiribati were settled as part of early equatorial migrations, whereas the easternmost atolls of Kiribati were only integrated into maritime circuits a millennium later during the rapid expansion of East Polynesian voyagers.
