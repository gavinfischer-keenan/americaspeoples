    Earliest People in Virginia

    The earliest people in Virginia were probably present by the late Pleistocene, and the strongest Virginia-specific evidence centers on Cactus Hill, where a possible pre-Clovis occupation lies below Clovis deposits Wagner2004Lothrop2016
    . The exact date is still debated, but published estimates for the earliest Cactus Hill occupation range to about 18,680–16,510 cal yr BP, while firmly recognized Paleoindian occupations in the broader literature are generally later, around or after 16,000 cal yr BP Lothrop2016
    Potter2021.
    Virginia Evidence

    Cactus Hill in southeastern Virginia is the main site cited for the earliest human presence in Virginia because it preserves stratified deposits with Clovis artifacts and an underlying apparent pre-Clovis blade component Wagner2004Boyd2016.

    Pedological work at Cactus Hill concluded that the pre-Clovis and Clovis materials occur in distinct buried horizons, with reported sterile sand separating them, which supports substantial deposit integrity even though some pedoturbation cannot be fully excluded Lothrop2016
    .

    Other important Virginia Paleoindian sites, such as Williamson and Catoctin Creek, show that people were certainly in Virginia during the Paleoindian period, but they do not currently provide an older, securely radiocarbon-dated occupation than the Cactus Hill claim Hill1998
    Dent1991.

    Broader Context

    Across North America, evidence for pre-Clovis occupations exists, including Page-Ladson in Florida at about 14,550 cal yr BP and Cooper’s Ferry in Idaho at 16,560–15,280 cal yr BP, which makes an early Virginia occupation plausible in principle Halligan2016
    Davis2019
    .

    At the same time, reviews of the peopling of the Americas stress that very early sites remain contested, and some claimed pre-Clovis or pre-LGM cases are considered unsubstantiated Potter2021Chatters2021.

    So, the earliest people in Virginia were likely there before Clovis, with Cactus Hill suggesting roughly 18,700–16,500 years ago, but the safest consensus is that Virginia was occupied by at least the late Paleoindian period, and the oldest proposed dates remain debated Lothrop2016
    Buchanan2007.
