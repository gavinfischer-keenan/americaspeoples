    This inquiry encompasses two core dimensions: the earliest chronological and material evidence of human occupation in Brazil, and the biological and genetic relationships connecting those early populations to present-day Indigenous groups.
    Earliest Evidence of Human Occupation

    Genomic Affinities with Modern Indigenous Peoples

    Despite craniometric discrepancies, genome-wide ancient DNA confirms that early Holocene Brazilian populations share direct genetic ancestry with modern Indigenous Americans Strauss2020Santos2022Ferraz2023+1 more.
