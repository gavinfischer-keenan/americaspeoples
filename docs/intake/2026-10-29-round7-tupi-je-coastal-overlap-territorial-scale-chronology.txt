    What “How Big” Means Here

    The literature is much stronger on distribution and frontiers than on exact territorial area Cruz2020
    Noelli2004Bonomo2015. For Tupi-Guarani, the clearest size estimate is continental: expansions reached over 2,500–4,000 km across lowland South America, with settlement following major rivers and later the coast Loponte2025Iriarte2017
    . For Guaraní specifically, southern coastal occupation began late in your window, with dates in Rio Grande do Sul near the Atlantic coast by 570–718 AD and clearer littoral settlement by 930–980 AD Bonomo2015
    .

    For Macro-Jê, the evidence points to clustered territorial landscapes: permanent high-density settlement areas, pit-house zones, dark-earth villages up to about 4,000 m², and ceremonial earthworks 20–200 m across Noelli2017Iriarte2016
    . Those are site-scale measures, not total polity area, so the safest conclusion is that Macro-Jê coastal territories were smaller, discontinuous, and ecologically anchored, while Tupi coastal territories were broader and more corridor-based Cruz2020
    Noelli2004
    Iriarte2017
    .

    So, during AD 0–1000, Tupi and Macro-Jê territories did overlap, but mainly in restricted coastal-frontier corridors rather than across the whole shoreline. The Tupi sphere was larger at the macro-regional scale, while Macro-Jê territories were more segmented and centered on highland-coastal transition zones.
