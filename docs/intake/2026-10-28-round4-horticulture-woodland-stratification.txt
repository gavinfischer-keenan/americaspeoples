    Household Production and Emerging Inequality

    Late Archaic foraging bands domesticated indigenous seed crops around 3,500 to 5,000 years ago, initiating a long-term transition from broad foraging to intensive plant husbandry Hollenbach2022Watson2020Mueller2018. Computational models and architectural analyses across more than 800 residential structures show that dietary broadening toward mass-harvested crops lowered the productive age of children, relaxing constraints on domestic group size and generating right-tailed distributions of household wealth White2013. These compounding demographic advantages laid the basis for inherited status differences that materialized in later Woodland horizons Bender1985White2013.
