    Earliest Middle Holocene Habitation

    The oldest reported human context in Cuba and the insular Caribbean is the Canímar Abajo shell-matrix site, located along the Canímar River in Matanzas Province De Armas2024
    Roksandic2015. A radiocarbon charcoal sample from the basal shell midden (UNAM-0715) produced a date of cal BC 5480–5380, identifying it as the earliest dated occupational locus on the island De Armas2024
    Roksandic2015. Direct accelerator mass spectrometry (AMS) dating of human skeletal collagen demonstrates that the older cemetery was established between cal BC 2237 and 790, representing the oldest directly dated human remains in the Antilles De Armas2024
    Roksandic2015. Isotopic profiles from tooth enamel reveal that these early inhabitants maintained surprisingly low residential mobility, relying heavily on local estuarine resources rather than functioning solely as transient foragers De Armas2024
    .

    Across western Cuba, early habitation deposits dating to ca. 5000 BC yield flaked-stone tools and Archaic ground-stone blades Keegan2017Keegan2016. These early lithic assemblages closely resemble early stone technologies documented in Belize and the Yucatan Peninsula, supporting colonization models across the Yucatan Channel Keegan2016. The absence of comparable early preceramic material in Jamaica before 500 BC or in Florida suggests direct mainland-to-Antillean navigation routes into western Cuba rather than stepping-stone movements from southeastern North America Keegan2016Callaghan2008.
