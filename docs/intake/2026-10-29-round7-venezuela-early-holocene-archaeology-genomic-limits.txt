    Archaeological Proof of Early Holocene Occupation

    Excavations in southern and eastern Venezuela verify continuous human settlement extending directly into the early Holocene Scaramelli2017
    . At the Cerro Gavilán 2 rockshelter in Bolívar State, accelerator mass spectrometry (AMS) radiocarbon assays from excavated stratified deposits established occupations dating from 9,250 ± 60 BP to 3,440 ± 40 BP Scaramelli2017. These early Holocene components contain unifacial flake tools alongside preserved floral and charred faunal remains, documenting generalized subsistence in tropical forest margins Scaramelli2017. Similarly, stratified investigations near Puerto Ayacucho in the Venezuelan Amazon documented preceramic lithic assemblages extending back into the 9th millennium BP Scaramelli2017
    .

    These early Holocene sites represent local adaptations following older Late Pleistocene megafaunal hunting complexes established at northwestern Venezuelan sites like Taima-Taima and Muaco between ~19,810 and 15,780 cal yr BP Carlini2022
    Potter2021. Rather than abandoning the landscape after megafaunal extinctions, foragers shifted toward sylvan subsistence strategies Cooke1998. During the Pleistocene-Holocene boundary (ca. 11,700–8,200 cal BP), populations across northern South America replaced bifacial flaking and fluted projectile points with unifacial toolkits, wild plant collection, and small-game hunting Cooke1998Ranere2020.
