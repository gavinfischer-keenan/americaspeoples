    Genomic Evidence for Regional Continuity

    Whole-genome and mitogenome analyses confirm that ancient individuals recovered from maritime contexts along the Pacific Northwest Coast exhibit ancestor-descendant links to contemporary Indigenous groups Lindo2016Aqil2023Cui2013. The ~10,300 cal yr BP male individual known as Shuká Káa from On Your Knees Cave demonstrates 10,000 years of continuity along the northern coast, remaining basal to three ancient British Columbia coast individuals dating between ~6,075 and 1,750 cal yr BP Lindo2016Lindo2016. Genomic modeling also shows that late Pleistocene population structure existed prior to the diversification of inland groups, positioning Shuká Káa on an ancestral lineage distinct from Clovis-associated remains like Anzick-1 Lindo2016Lindo2016.
