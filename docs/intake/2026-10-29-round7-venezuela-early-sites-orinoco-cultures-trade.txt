    Earliest Human Occupation and Modern Genetic Ties

    The earliest traces of human activity in Venezuela come from the arid Falcón Basin, notably the spring sites of Taima-Taima and Muaco Carlini2022
    . At Taima-Taima, archaeologists recovered lanceolate El Jobo projectile point fragments directly embedded within the pelvic cavity of a juvenile mastodon (gomphothere) alongside other modified megafaunal bones, dated between ~17,300 and 15,780 cal yr BP Carlini2022
    . CT scans of fossil Glyptotherium glyptodont skulls demonstrate targeted cranial fractures made by percussive blows, indicating deliberate hunting strategies Carlini2022Carlini2022. Subsequent Early Holocene hunter-gatherer occupations are documented at rockshelters such as Cerro Gavilán 2 from ~9250 BP Scaramelli2017.
