    Pleistocene and Early Holocene Horizons

    Early presence is verified at the submerged Page-Ladson site, where stratified deposits date human butchery or scavenging of mastodon to ~14,550 cal yr BP Halligan2016
    . These foragers coexisted with Pleistocene megafauna for roughly two millennia before extinction around 12,600 cal yr BP Halligan2016
    . Because rising post-glacial sea levels inundated over half of the peninsula, much of the early record survives in submerged river channels and sinkhole basins like the Aucilla River Halligan2022Halligan2016
    .

    Terrestrial and inundated sites document a progression through Clovis, Suwannee, and Simpson lanceolate tool complexes centered on freshwater springs and chert quarries Faught2019Finn2012Daniel2021. In the Early Holocene, Side-notched Bolen points reflect population expansion and localized aggregations around persistent water sources and high-grade lithics Faught2019Halligan2023. Submerged finds in Apalachee Bay confirm logistical foraging occurred on ancient continental shelf territories that are now drowned Faught2004.
    Archaic Wetland and Coastal Adaptations
        Environmental stabilization around 5,000 years ago formed modern wetland ecosystems, enabling settlement of interior tree islands such as Heartleaf Hammock by 4,400 years ago Fradkin2016.
        Coastal settlements targeted protected elevated landforms with access to fresh water from ponds and rich marine fisheries McFadden2016.
        Intensive riverine exploitation in northwest Florida is evidenced by over 700 projectile points documenting dense pre-contact settlement throughout the Chipola River Valley Kreiser2018.
        Broad cultural configurations in South Florida endured for nearly ten millennia, developing distinct adaptations below the frost line McGoun2010.

    During the Woodland period, centers like Crystal River transformed from ceremonial aggregation sites into sedentary villages organized around central plazas Jackson2020Rodning2019. These societies balanced egalitarian collective governance against emergent social hierarchies Rodning2019. By late prehistory, stratified polities constructed monumental platform mounds, elevated shell ridges against coastal storms, and participated in Southeastern trade networks until European contact decimated indigenous populations Wallis2014Ewen1995Odum2018+1 more.

    This archaeological record illustrates an ongoing human adaptation to dynamic subtropical landscapes, sea-level changes, and evolving social networks over 14,000 years.
