    Taphonomic and Chronological Discontinuities
        Paleoindian fluted point occupations are reported from over 50 California locations, yet reliable radiocarbon assays are virtually absent between 12,900 and 12,200 cal BP, creating a visible cultural unconformity during the Younger Dryas Jones2008.
        Only two mainland archaeological sites preserve fluted points and early Holocene residues within the same stratigraphic sequence, and none of the 38 sites dated between 10,500 and 9,000 cal BP contain fluted points Jones2008.
        Postglacial marine transgressions submerged late Pleistocene shorelines by 80 to 85 meters, placing early open-coast settlements underwater and biasing the coastal record toward elevated upland sites Braje2022Reeder-Myers2015Erlandson2020
        .
        Deeply buried early Holocene sites along the Sacramento River and in the western Great Basin indicate that lack of detection reflects alluvial sedimentation rather than genuine demographic abandonment Smith2022
        Ugan2024.

    Archaeological continuity across the Pleistocene-Holocene boundary is clearly demonstrated in insular and coastal refugia, whereas the interior mainland record exhibits pronounced chronological gaps that reflect climatic disturbances or landscape evolution Jones2008Ugan2024Erlandson2020
