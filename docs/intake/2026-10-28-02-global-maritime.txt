Mechanisms Across Major Oceanic Theaters

In the Americas, the coastal migration theory posits that Upper Paleolithic hunter-gatherers followed maritime resources southward along the Pacific Rim Potter2018
Davis2020Erlandson2008. Ecological models suggest that continuous kelp forest ecosystems provided linear highways with uniform foraging resources from northeast Asia to South America Potter2018
Erlandson2017. However, empirical debates persist: while terminal Pleistocene sites on California's Channel Islands verify sophisticated seafaring by 13,000 calibrated years ago, securely dated coastal sites prior to 12,600 years ago remain scarce along preserved unglaciated northwestern shorelines Potter2018
Erlandson2008. Consequently, while coastal migration remains viable, researchers caution that current evidence cannot fully rule out interior routes through the ice-free corridor Potter2018.

In the Indo-Pacific, computer simulations show that successful colonization demanded deliberate maritime strategies Montenegro2016Anderson2008
. For the colonization of Sahul, oceanographic models indicate that accidental drift from southern Wallacea had a low probability of success, whereas planned 4- to 7-day voyages with founding populations exceeding 70 individuals easily reached emergent offshore shelves Bird2018
. Farther east, global biogeographical syntheses confirm that the colonization of Remote Oceania occurred much later because navigating large open-ocean gaps fundamentally required the innovation of sailing technology Anderson2006
