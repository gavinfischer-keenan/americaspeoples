    Early Holocene Transcontinental Baselines

    The earliest documented evidence of long-distance east-west exchange appears at the Pleistocene-Holocene boundary Fitzgerald2005O'Shea2021
    . Marine shell beads recovered from dry lake basins establish an early connection across western ecological barriers Fitzgerald2005. Radiocarbon determinations on eleven Olivella biplicata spire-lopped beads found 250 to 365 kilometers inland from the Pacific coast date between 11,200 and 7860 cal BP, proving established trade with the southwestern Great Basin by at least 10,300 to 10,000 cal BP Fitzgerald2005.

    Transcontinental movement of lithic raw materials extended across even wider geographical spans during the early Holocene O'Shea2021
    Dillian2009. Compositional analysis of two obsidian flakes dating to ~9000 BP recovered beneath Lake Huron matches the Wagontire source in central Oregon, demonstrating that volcanic glass crossed approximately 4,000 kilometers from the Pacific Northwest to the Great Lakes O'Shea2021
    . This find represents the earliest and furthest eastward movement of western obsidian recorded in North America, likely facilitated by personal, down-the-line networks alongside early watercraft travel O'Shea2021
