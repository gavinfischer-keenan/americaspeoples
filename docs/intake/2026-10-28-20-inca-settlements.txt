    Urban Typologies and Regional Variations

    Inca urban expressions varied noticeably between ecozones. On the coast, Inca administrators conquered established urban kingdoms like Chimor, incorporating dense mud-brick cities directly into Tawantinsuyu Moore2018Fagan2021. In the highlands, urbanism followed earlier Middle Horizon precedents set by Wari and Tiwanaku Moore2018Fagan2021. Rather than building dense commercial metropolises everywhere, the Inca frequently constructed specialized civic-ceremonial towns centered around expansive plazas, storerooms, and military installations Presta2023Ferrari2021
    .

    In frontier valleys such as Copiapó in Chile and the Calchaquí Valley in Argentina, state presence progressed through phased interventions Garrido2024Presta2023. These interventions ranged from high-altitude sanctuaries to planned administrative compounds containing massive halls (kallankas) Garrido2024Ferrari2021
    . Farther north in the Ecuadorian Andes, surveys in the País Caranqui document direct coercive outposts engineered to disrupt local ties and enforce state control Bray1992.
