    Figure 1 Research consensus on pre-5000 cal BP occupations across the ABC islands

    New accelerator mass spectrometry (AMS) dates on charcoal from the C-1426 rockshelter at Saliña Sint Marie extend Curaçao's occupation back to 5735–5600 cal BP, roughly 290–850 years earlier than the previously established settlement chronology Kraan2024. The Curaçao Cultural Landscape Project's archaeological and palaeoecological investigations at Rif Sint Marie and Jan Thiel corroborate a 5700-year settlement history on the island Giovas2026. Bayesian colonization models that applied chronometric hygiene to 2,484 Caribbean radiocarbon determinations place Curaçao in the earliest settlement cluster (ca. 5800–2500 cal BP), consistent with its close proximity to mainland South America Napolitano2019
    . Broader regional syntheses confirm that the Antilles were settled much earlier than once thought, around 7,000 cal yr BP, in multiple waves showing strong linkages to South America Fitzpatrick2015.
    Archaic Burials and Early Settlement Evidence

    Archaic burial sites have been identified on both Aruba and Curaçao, including the Malmok cemetery site with more than 60 burials, the Canashitu rockshelter containing five individuals, and three individuals from the St. Michielsburg site Hackenberger2021
    . On Bonaire, radiocarbon dates indicate the island was first settled around 1500 BC (~3500 cal BP), marking the beginning of the Archaic Age there Seferidou2024
    . A zooarchaeological synthesis of 26 sites across the ABC islands documents human exploitation of local ecosystems from ca. 4000 BC onward Hackenberger2021. These early foraging groups, who used stone and shell technology, impacted island landscapes as evidenced by bird and sloth extinctions Fitzpatrick2007.
