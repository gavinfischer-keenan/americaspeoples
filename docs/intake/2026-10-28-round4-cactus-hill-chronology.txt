    The archaeological community remains split over whether the deposits at Cactus Hill demonstrate an undisturbed pre-Clovis human occupation, as stratigraphic integrity and dating methods are actively debated. Figure 1 Scientific consensus on pre-Clovis occupation evidence at Cactus Hill

    Cactus Hill, situated along the Nottoway River in southeastern Virginia, is among the most prominent candidate sites for human presence in eastern North America prior to the Clovis complex Feathers2006Goodyear2005Hamilton2007
    . The primary archaeological interest centers on a stratified cultural sequence where an assemblage of stone blades lies beneath diagnostic Clovis artifacts Feathers2006Wagner2004Stokstad2000.
    Stratigraphy and Site Integrity

    The cultural deposits at Cactus Hill are set within a relic sand dune characterized by multisequal eolian deposits Feathers2006Wagner2004Macphail2008. The basal sand layer overlies an ancient paleosol dated to approximately 19,540 ± 70 radiocarbon years before present and is culturally sterile Wagner2004.

    Directly overlying this basal stratum is an eolian sand unit containing both the Clovis horizon and an underlying blade level, which are associated with distinct surface horizons and clay lamellae Wagner2004Macphail2008. Micromorphological analysis indicates that the sedimentary sequence has been broadly stable over the long term, showing only minor post-depositional bioturbation and distinguishing the occupation levels from intervening sterile sands Macphail2008. However, geological evidence also reveals deflation, low accretion rates, and small-scale turbation that have compressed the sedimentary profile Feathers2006Macphail2008.

    Geochronology and Age Estimates

    Luminescence dating using optically stimulated luminescence (OSL) on single quartz and feldspar grains placed the sand deposit enclosing the pre-Clovis artifacts at approximately 18 ka Feathers2006. These OSL determinations broadly align with radiocarbon assays from the lower horizon, with almost all ages conforming to correct stratigraphic order Feathers2006.

    In contrast, the overlying Clovis occupation at Cactus Hill has been refined by multiple radiocarbon dates on hearth charcoal, averaging 10,860 ± 20 14C yr B.P. or roughly 12,820 to 12,745 cal yr B.P. Waters2020
    . Diagnostic Clovis horizons across North America consistently constrain Clovis chronology to roughly 13,050 to 12,750 cal yr B.P. Waters2007Waters2020

    Archaeological Debates and Caveats

    Despite supporting geological analyses, the pre-Clovis designation at Cactus Hill remains disputed among many archaeologists Feathers2006Goodyear2005Mathers2023+1 more. Skepticism centers on the sandy substrate, as eolian dunes are vulnerable to bioturbation and vertical artifact migration, which can cause older sediment dates to artificially associate with younger lithic items Feathers2006Stokstad2000.

    Wider regional syntheses also express caution, noting that while strict "Clovis-First" models are insufficient, evidence for occupations preceding 16,000 cal yr B.P. across North America requires exceptionally rigorous taphonomic demonstration Waters2007Potter2021. Sites such as Saltville and Topper have also faced critical scrutiny regarding contextual associations and the cultural origin of lithic assemblages Goodyear2005. Consequently, while Cactus Hill offers compelling evidence for an early techno-complex in the mid-Atlantic, researchers continue to evaluate its chronological integrity against broader models of late Pleistocene colonization Goodyear2005Hamilton2007Potter2021.
