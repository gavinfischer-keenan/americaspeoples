    Humans have inhabited Micronesia for ~3500 years, driving distinct maritime dispersal streams.

    Archaeological, linguistic, and genomic evidence addresses human antiquity in Micronesia across three distinct sub-questions: absolute settlement antiquity, successive dispersal streams, and long-distance exchange connectivity.
