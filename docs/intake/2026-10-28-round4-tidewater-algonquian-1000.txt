    Archaeological Sequence and Settlement

    Tidewater Virginia preserves a deep record of continuous Algonquian occupation spanning the Middle Woodland, Late Woodland, and early colonial periods Gallivan2024Gallivan2016Herlich2016. Settlement studies along the lower York River demonstrate that the dispersed village model characteristic of later Powhatan towns emerged during the Mockley Phase (AD 200–900) and persisted into the second millennium Gallivan2024Richmond2016. At the Kiskiak site, continuous stratified shell middens and domestic features reflect steady, long-term riverine settlement directly encompassing 1000 CE Gallivan2024Herlich2016.

    During this interval, regional communities increasingly concentrated their settlements along productive estuarine streams and shifted toward more sedentary lifeways Gallivan2024
    Herlich2016Ethridge2017. This occupation sequence connects directly to later political centers such as Werowocomoco, where extensive precontact earthworks and spatial organizations were developed over centuries prior to European contact Gallivan2016Gallivan2007.
