    Analytical Methodologies for Sourcing

    Geochemical fingerprinting has increasingly complemented traditional macroscopic inspection to achieve high-resolution provenance of lithic raw materials Favreau2022Ekshtain2014Morisaki2024+1 more. While non-destructive optical microscopy and petrographic thin-section analyses effectively identify microfossils, texture, and mineral composition from reference collections, they are often paired with elemental assays to resolve inter-source geochemical overlap Favreau2022Beller2023Kadowaki2026+1 more. Laser ablation inductively coupled plasma mass spectrometry (LA-ICP-MS) and solution ICP-MS/AES successfully discriminate trace element signatures among chert, flint, and limestone formations Beller2023Brandl2018Ekshtain2014.

    For volcanic and sedimentary toolstones, non-destructive portable X-ray fluorescence (pXRF) and energy dispersive X-ray fluorescence (EDXRF) provide rapid characterization of trace and rare earth elements Nutman2025Egeland2019Finestone2025
    +2 more. Multivariate statistical frameworks, such as principal component analysis and discriminant functions, classify geological source samples with 75% to 80% accuracy based on immobile element ratios Nash2022Ekshtain2014Egeland2019+2 more.

    Technological Decisions and Reduction Strategies

    Raw material procurement models distinguish between direct acquisition—targeted trips organized exclusively to harvest stone—and embedded procurement, where toolstone is acquired incidentally during seasonal subsistence tasks Will2026
    . While extensive extraction pits and quarry sites exist across prehistoric landscapes, early forager assemblages primarily reflect embedded collection from both bedrock outcrops and redeposited river gravels Mester2022
    Brandl2018Lesage2022
    +1 more.

    Distance to source directly conditions lithic reduction sequences and curation intensity Ekshtain2014Jochim2024
    . Local materials typically enter sites as minimally prepared cores or large tabular blanks that undergo intensive on-site reduction, leaving heavy debitage and cortical flakes McHenry2018Ekshtain2014Kadowaki2026
    . In contrast, distant exotic materials are predominantly imported as finished bifaces, highly curated formal tools, or prepared bladelet cores to economize transport mass Nash2022Ekshtain2014Martín-Jarque2024
    +1 more. Furthermore, hunter-gatherer selectivity often extended beyond strict mechanical utility, incorporating thermal alteration to improve flaking predictability and prioritizing distinct stone colors or vitreous textures for social and symbolic expression McHenry2018Egeland2019
