# Pacific connections, places and changing boundaries

## Intent and authorization
Extend the educational Watch / Explore atlas with source-backed Pacific connections from mainland Asia through Australia and the islands; Indigenous settlements before European encounters; Caribbean colonial and earlier towns; violence evidence even when actors are unknown; changing political boundaries and forced removals. User previously delegated design/implementation judgment and now explicitly requests these additions. Carry out implementation without another design approval gate. Production publication is a separate release decision.

## Evidence rules
- Maximum **100 Consensus tool calls total**, including status polls; all research dispatches use **Deep**. Maintain a ledger. Preserve returned research documents and citations verbatim in outputs. Newly added user topics may receive their own complete Deep brief; do not chain self-generated research steps.
- No claim of every city, every violent event or complete global coverage. Provide counts, a geographic/chronological coverage matrix and an actionable missing-data queue.
- Founding, first surviving evidence, first European encounter, renaming, conquest, rebuilding and relocation are distinct. Native settlements begin at supported earlier dates, not first colonial mention. Ongoing habitation is explicitly clipped at 1934, not ended there.
- Routes animate only supported intervals. Distinguish positive exchange evidence, latest observed imports, inferred cessation and documented closure; infer neither bidirectionality nor annual traffic. Unknown direction gets no directional arrow. Broad dating remains a range; any preferred viewing date is an editorial estimate, not a new fact.
- Abandonment requires positive local evidence. Allow bounded approximate abandonment/relocation, retaining historic markers and uncertainty. Rapa Nui must not be labeled wholly abandoned from demographic or monument-building changes.
- Violence distinguishes lethal evidence, healed/nonlethal trauma, uncertain cause, ritual killing, massacre and accumulated cemetery samples. Unknown perpetrators remain unknown. Do not interpret burnt buildings or abandonment alone as attacks.
- Locations use cited public site locators where possible, otherwise named-town/island anchors with stated uncertainty. Audit all existing records for obvious region mismatches and coarse placeholders, including Florida/New Hampshire and Barbados. Do not manufacture archaeological precision or publish sensitive exact graves.

## Presentation
Keep Watch / Explore / Library. Add source-linked guides and journeys where new data makes them useful. Extend the default Pacific frame west to mainland East/Southeast Asia and south to all Australia and Aotearoa; add regional presets. Improve coastline detail for small islands/local zoom without pretending to reconstruct ancient sea levels.

Political lines are a **separate dated background layer**, dotted, no fill, with readable historic name labels, non-intercepting pointers so city selection remains first. Controls select layers and offer a source/chronology list accessible by keyboard. Only boundaries applying to the selected map year appear, even in all-period Explore; the UI must always disclose that year. Use end-of-year snapshots for day-dated changes and document this choice. A boundary end replaces the old extent, rather than accumulating incompatible outlines. Optional earlier purchase/cession context must be explicitly distinguished from current administrative boundaries.

Boundary categories: acquisition/cession, colonial claim/administration, territory, state, reservation. Start from Newberry's dated state/territory geometry, authoritative reusable island colonial outlines, and supported reservation versions; expand where primary geometry exists. Louisiana Purchase precedes its subdivided territories/states. Treaty/claim outlines do not establish actual control or extinguishment of Indigenous presence; cessions are not reservations. Do not back-project present borders or interpolate unsupported boundaries. Retain precise provenance, date and geometry limits for every version.

Forced removals are source-linked directional routes with supported windows and explicit coercion, distinct from voluntary migrations. Show separate paths/episodes where documented; use schematic endpoints only when a reconstructed route is not available. Connect changed reservation boundaries with relocation events only if evidence supports that connection.

## Architecture / alternatives
Chosen: separate boundary collection and renderer, plus additive evidence records and backward-compatible history metadata. This keeps filled contextual research from becoming sovereignty and avoids altering visitor drafts. Rejected: reuse filled cultural circles as territories (not boundaries); import current borders then apply historic dates (anachronistic); animate guesses between sparse political snapshots (invented changes).

Existing evidence model, source reviews, API catalog, workbook export and validation remain the integration path. New records retain full claim/source support and meaningful uncertainty. Boundary data stays separate from population counts and city counts. Detailed geography is a same-origin packaged asset with graceful fallback.

## Builder discretion

Prefer correct broad extents over false precision. Explicitly report regions and geometry awaiting better sources. Test timeline boundaries, source support, chronology gaps, movement directions, right-censored habitation, overlap picking with political lines, dateline routes, expanded frame, mobile controls, export/import and self-host packaging.

## Added voyage and mission requirements
User subsequently requested major expeditions and ships: Beagle/Darwin, Magellan–Elcano, Lewis/Clark, Norse journeys, and other major Americas/Pacific expeditions; and the 21 Alta California mission foundations, with Baja additions where supported. Itineraries must preserve arrival/departure dates and stops. Display dotted route legs during the journey, a moving ship during dated sea travel and held position at stops. Show a traveler/canoe rather than a sea ship for mixed/land routes. Approximate saga evidence must not imply a daily logged track. A playback view may compress time but must disclose the displayed date and stop; no invented port order or daily position. Lines between verified stops are explicitly schematic. Port visit markers accumulate as historical visits, separate from city foundations. Mission dates denote institutions, not necessarily construction or present-site occupation; encode documented moves separately. Include source-supported Indigenous context/coercion. San Diego 1542 refers to Cabrillo, not Balboa; Balboa's 1513 isthmus crossing is a separate land expedition.

## Added transport and scope constraints
User added Pony Express, transcontinental/eastern rail expansion, and railways in Latin America, Hawaii and Alaska, through 1934. Show only documented built or under-construction infrastructure, never proposed/announced lines. When construction dates are unavailable, reveal a segment at its verified opening, not earlier. If a closure is unknown, do not silently promise service forever; retain physical-build history separately from evidence of operation.

Detailed study area is the Americas, Pacific islands and Australia. Mainland Asia and Europe serve as generalized entry origins; no detailed settlements or political histories there are required. Incoming lines are labeled approximate source regions, not invented departure towns. European ports in a documented expedition itinerary may provide off-map endpoints without turning Europe into a new atlas region.

No delegated Consensus calls; the primary agent keeps the single call ledger. Final whole-branch review remains fresh and independent.
