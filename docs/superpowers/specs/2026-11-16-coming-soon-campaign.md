## Evidence and behavior

- Supported settlements, dated local activities, encounters and routes enter the master map, with reciprocal links to existing records. Topic-only language and commodity details remain focused.
- A date in a contextual story remains the date everywhere. Independent topics retain their own clock and restore the shared map on return.
- Dates distinguish occupation, construction, attestation and uncertain hypotheses. Gaps are not abandonment. Linguistic descent is not a genetic or political boundary. No imagined exact building footprints, routes or ethnicity.
- Source checking follows the completed whole-campaign Consensus Deep dossier with original scholarship and institutional/community sources. Raw Consensus output stays private. Cumulative usage is 74/100, polls included; no additional calls are needed for this campaign.
- The master endpoint stays 1934. The prior Hawaiʻi-only exception stays isolated.
- Available entries replace planned cards rather than duplicate them. Existing guide/movie links remain usable. Computer/tablet layouts are primary.
- Each batch is tested, independently reviewed once, merged and deployed, with the current release and one previous rollback retained. Historical evidence remains incomplete; completion means a substantive dedicated treatment, not every known site or claim.

## Source quality

The dossier is a discovery aid, not authority for claims. Check its references: mismatched Chan Chan citation, Tiwanaku chronology, DOI errors, and context-dependent Teotihuacan burning dates have already required correction. Save access limits and competing interpretations with the records. Broad public site anchors are labeled as such; scholarly claim support does not imply surveyed geometry.
