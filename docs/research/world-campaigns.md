# Campaign-study research and geometry audit

Reviewed 18 November 2026. This is a local data contribution; nothing was deployed.

The two studies each contain 17 evidence anchors and seven dated campaign connections. Genghis Khan has four source-derived territory versions; Hannibal has nine Carthaginian-polity versions. Guides are in the existing native guide schema, with every paragraph, table row and mapped point linked to its bibliography.

## Chronology and location decisions

- Historical BCE is converted to integer astronomical year `1 − BCE`: 219 BCE is −218; 202 BCE is −201. Cliopatria’s README uses signed BCE/CE, so its negative source years receive `+1`.
- Genghis Khan’s core reign is 1206–1227. Karakorum in 1235 is explicitly successor context. The Avraga point retains the much broader published radiocarbon envelope, approximately the mid-twelfth through fifteenth centuries; it is not a continuous-occupation assertion or the study’s principal playback window.
- No exact Onon assembly site, Alpine pass, Rhône crossing, Ticinus/Metaurus battlefield, Indus crossing, Zama battlefield, or Genghis death/burial location is invented. The coordinates are locality or regional anchors. Unknown routes remain schematic and all wave descriptions say so.
- Córdoba’s elephant bone retains the fourth–third-century BCE radiocarbon range (represented as 400–201 BCE), separate from its proposed Second Punic War context. The 2026 publication date never becomes an occupation date. This animal is not identified as one of Hannibal’s Alpine elephants.
- Cartagena’s 229 BCE foundation follows the named archaeological museum chronology; the point describes its selected Punic urban phase through the 209 BCE capture. The guide does not present the foundation date as an independently measured archaeological event.
- Ancient Capua is anchored at Santa Maria Capua Vetere. Gurganj is anchored at Kunya-Urgench, not modern Urgench. The Tarentum narrative preserves the Roman-held citadel rather than implying undivided Carthaginian control.
- No numerical settlement populations were defensibly established from the consulted sources. Population arrays are omitted. Army strengths, literary casualties and Roman citizen registrations are not silently converted into city populations.

## Geometry provenance

Source: [Cliopatria pinned commit](https://github.com/Seshat-Global-History-Databank/cliopatria/tree/5f433377139a6eeaeacbdc894b70f8805b72c8b5), archive SHA-256 `e10a4e429fca708788ff9e7572a95fce801fae55852d217cffabc1c59cd4eed4`. The original licence was verified locally and on its official repository page. Source and derivative geometry retain [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); the atlas’s separate noncommercial terms do not replace that licence.

The preparation script requires the pinned archive and original licence, keeps complete source polygon components, simplifies with topology preservation at 0.08 degrees, rounds coordinates to five decimals, orients rings for D3, and rejects invalid geometry. It does not form point hulls, clip to modern national borders, or interpolate new frontiers. Every output records its source row and original date interval in the visible limits text.

Mongol rows: 5272 (1206–1209), 5364 (1210–1215), 5430 (1216–1219), 5466 (1220–1226). Row 5505 (1227–1235) is deliberately omitted because its interval spans successor conquests and cannot establish the personal reign’s final frontier. The missing 1227 outline is a source-selection gap, not political disappearance.

Carthage rows: 679, 733, 735, 746, 758, 767, 800, 817, 823, restricted to 219–202 BCE. These represent the historical-atlas Carthaginian polity, not Hannibal’s personal occupation of every place in Italy. The final source window may combine wartime and treaty adjustments. Source windows are coarse reconstructions, not a complete annual record of authority.

Reproduce from the existing research cache:

```powershell
python scripts/prepare-world-campaign-territories.py --source-dir ../pacific-research/cliopatria --python-libs ../pacific-research/python-libs
```

The script updates only `data/world/campaigns.json` territory arrays and study territory references. It requires Shapely, already available in the cited cache; it performs no network request.

## Verification

Combined `validateWorldData` through `mergeWorldShards` passed with all current research shards, including cross-shard Homo sapiens references and all study links. `readWorldGuides()` validated both new guides, including their 34 coordinates, citations and table row shapes. The geometry preparation verified the archive fingerprint and licence, retained 13 selected source versions, and checked valid nonempty polygon geometry after simplification and rounding.

The runtime, overview cards, guide-index generation and terrain rendering are integrated by the main task. Those browser/visual checks are not claimed here.
