# Africa, Europe & Asia–Pacific to 1900 CE and UNESCO Deep Dives

Research and access check: 20 November 2026.

## Overview

Across `data/world/`, eight coordinated shards pair Consensus API academic literature with trusted UNESCO World Heritage, museum, university and expedition sources to cover **Africa**, **Europe**, and **Asia–Pacific** from the earliest hominin fossils cleanly through **1900 CE**, eliminate sparse chronological windows (`min >= 4` in all 26 post-`15,000 BCE` windows across all three continents), and add **21** regional and thematic Deep Dives in `data/atlases/africa/archaeology-sites-deep-dives.json`:

1. `data/world/holocene-africa-north-nile-sahara.json` (**68** records, **3** waves, **170** sources): Covers Maghreb & Cyrenaica (`Taforalt`, `Afalou Bou Rhummel`, `Tamar Hat`, `Haua Fteah`, `Capsian`, `Kaf Taht el-Ghar`, `Carthage`, `Cirta/Tipasa`, `Timgad/Sabratha/Cyrene`, `Volubilis`, `Leptis Magna`, `Kairouan`, `Sijilmasa & M'Zab`, `Fez`, `Marrakesh`, `Rabat & Tinmel`, `Ait-Ben-Haddou`, `Qalʿat Banī Ḥammād & Tlemcen`, `Hafsid Tunis & Ottoman Algiers`), Green Sahara & Oasis Networks (`Ounjougou`, `Gobero`, `Nabta Playa`, `Gilf Kebir`, `Ennedi`, `Takarkori`, `Tadrart Acacus`, `Tassili n'Ajjer`, `Messak Settafet`, `Garama`, `Chinguetti/Ouadane/Oualata`, `Agadez`, `Ghadamès/Zuwīla`, `Touat/Gourara foggaras`, `Trans-Saharan Caravan Wave`), Nile Valley, Nubia, Funj, Darfur & Red Sea (`Wadi Kubbaniya`, `Elkab & Esna`, `Jebel Sahaba`, `Al-Khiday`, `Khor Shambat`, `Faiyum`, `Merimde`, `Saïs`, `Hierakonpolis`, `Abydos`, `Memphis/Saqqara/Dahshur`, `Giza`, `Thebes/Karnak/Luxor`, `Amarna`, `Abu Simbel & Philae`, `Kerma`, `Barkal/Napata`, `Dangeil`, `Meroë`, `Old Dongola`, `Faras`, `Soba East`, `Sennar Funj Sultanate`, `Qasr Ibrim`, `Uri & Ain Farah Darfur`, `Suakin & ʿAydhāb`, `Fusṭāṭ & Cairo`), and Hijaz sanctuaries (`Mecca`, `Medina`).
