# Africa Holocene to 1900 CE, Mecca, Faiths, and Mediterranean Trade

Research and access check: 20 November 2026.

## Overview

Prior to this expansion, the Africa continental view (`#atlas/africa`) contained 161 records of which 156 ended at or before 15,000 BCE (`#atlas/africa/early`) and only five records fell between 15,000 BCE and 1900 CE (`spread-shum-laka-early`, `spread-bantu-expansion-model`, `spread-shum-laka-late`, `hannibal-zama`, `silk-berenike`). Three African Holocene-to-1900 CE shards plus a cross-regional Mediterranean maritime trade shard bring `#atlas/africa` after 15,000 BCE to **160** evidence anchors (**139** overlapping the **10,000 BCE – 1000 CE** timeline across all African subregions, plus **49** continuing or dated into **1001 – 1900 CE**) alongside **nine** new Deep Dives:

1. `data/world/holocene-africa-north-nile-sahara.json` (**42** records, **2** waves, **94** sources): Iberomaurusian & Capsian Maghreb (`Taforalt`, `Haua Fteah`, `Eastern Maghreb Capsian`, `Kaf Taht el-Ghar`, `Jebel Sahaba`), Green Sahara massifs & pastoralism (`Gobero`, `Nabta Playa & Bir Kiseiba`, `Gilf Kebir & Wadi Sura`, `Ennedi`, `Takarkori`, `Tassili n'Ajjer`, `Messak Settafet`), Predynastic to Pharaonic Egypt & Delta (`Faiyum`, `Merimde Beni Salama`, `Saïs`, `Hierakonpolis`, `Abydos Tomb U-j`, `Giza`, `Amarna`), Nubia, Atbai & Kush (`Middle Nile kerma–kawa transition`, `Atbai rock art`, `Southern Atbai & Mahal Teglinos`, `Esh-Shaheinab`, `Kerma`, `Jebel Barkal & Napata`, `Dangeil`, `Meroë`), Garamantes & North African/Maghreb urban networks (`Garama / Jarma`, `Tangier`, `Carthage`, `Cirta & Tipasa`, `Volubilis / Walīla`, `Leptis Magna`), medieval Nubian & Islamic centres (`Old Dongola`, `Faras & Soba`, `Kairouan`, `Sijilmasa & M'Zab`, `Fusṭāṭ & Historic Cairo`, `Fez / Fès`), and the Hijaz–Red Sea corridor (`Mecca`, `Medina`).
2. `data/world/holocene-africa-west-central.json` (**39** records, **73** sources): Terminal Pleistocene / early ceramic and millet frontiers (`Iwo Eleru`, `Shum Laka Holocene`, `Bosumpra Cave`, `Ounjougou`, `Dufuna Canoe`, `Kintampo Complex`, `Gajiganna Complex`, `Dhar Tichitt & Dhar Néma`, `Karkarichinkat`), West African ironworking & first-millennium towns (`Nok Culture`, `Lejja`, `Dia Shoma & Mara`, `Lac de Guiers`, `Jenné-jeno`, `Kissi`, `Birnin Lafiya`, `Tongo Maaré Diabal`, `Essouk-Tadmekka`, `Gao Ancien & Gao Saney`, `Didé West 1`, `Senegambian Stone Circles`, `Igbo-Ukwu`, `Ilé-Ifẹ̀`, `Koumbi Saleh`), Central Africa (`Pikunda-Munda & Sangha`, `Lopé-Okanda`, `West-Central & Lower Congo pottery/iron`, `Imbonga / Idiofa`, `Upemba Depression`, `Congo Basin hiatus & recovery`), and West/Central states through 1900 CE (`Begho`, `Kano`, `Benin City`, `Mbanza Kongo`, `Elmina`, `Oyo-Ile`, `Abomey`, `Kumasi`).
3. `data/world/holocene-africa-east-south-madagascar.json` (**50** records, **1** wave, **100** sources): East African LSA, Pastoral Neolithic & Great Lakes (`Panga ya Saidi Holocene`, `Mumba & Nasera`, `SE Uganda / Lake Victoria`, `Kansyore`, `Kisese II & Mumba`, `Lothagam North`, `Luxmanda`, `Great Lakes Urewe & Kakapel`), Horn of Africa (`Goda Buticha`, `Laas Geel & Somaliland`, `Yeha`, `Matara`, `Adulis`, `Aksum`, `Beta Samati`, `Harlaa`, `Lalibela`, `Gondar`), Swahili Coast & Interior (`Unguja Ukuu`, `Shanga`, `Kilwa Kisiwani & Songo Mnara`, `Gedi`, `Chwaka`, `Ntusi & Bigo bya Mugenyi`, `Engaruka`), Southern Africa LSA, early herders & mixed farming (`Nelson Bay Cave`, `Matobo Hills`, `Spoegrivier`, `Leopard Cave`, `Tsodilo Hills`, `Seḥonghong`, `Toteng`, `Kalundu`, `Ndondondwane`, `Lotshitshi`, `Southern Kalahari / Karoo foragers`, `Kasteelberg`, `Little Muck & Skirbeek`, `Bosutswe`, `Chibuene`, `Schroda, K2 & Mapungubwe Hill`, `Mapela Hill`, `Great Zimbabwe`, `Khami`, `Bokoni`), and Comoros / Madagascar (`Dembeni`, `Sandrakatsy`, `Lakatoni Anja & Velondriake`, `Mahilaka`, `Highland Madagascar & Ambohimanga`).
4. `data/world/holocene-mediterranean-trade.json` (**28** records, **3** broad maritime-corridor waves, **43** sources): Shared cross-atlas cities, harbors, amphora depots, and Bronze–Medieval shipwrecks spanning North Africa, the Levant, Anatolia, Aegean, Italy, Iberia, and the Mediterranean–Red Sea corridor (`24` records in `#atlas/africa`, `27` in `#atlas/europe/family`, `14` in `#atlas/asia-pacific`), including `Byblos`, `Minoa / Knossos`, `Uluburun Wreck`, `Tyre`, `Cadiz / Gadir`, `Phoenician Sardinia & Motya`, `Cyrene & Apollonia`, `Syracuse / Ortygia`, `Naukratis & Thonis-Heracleion`, `Marseille / Massalia`, `Rhodes`, `Delos`, `Alexandria`, `Ostia & Portus`, `Puteoli`, `Palmyra`, `Caesarea Maritima`, `Ephesus`, `Clysma / Qulzum`, `Monte Testaccio`, `Baetica`, `Constantinople Harbors`, `Fustat / al-Mina`, `Serçe Limanı Wreck`, `Venice Lagoon`, `Amalfi`, `Mahdia & Palermo`, and `Pisa & Genoa`.

## Multi-source literature & institutional provenance

Consistent with the rest of the atlas, peer-reviewed scholarly literature is paired with trusted public institutional, UNESCO, museum, university GIS/3D survey, archaeological mission, radiocarbon, and gazetteer sources:

- **Consensus scholarly literature (`275` distinct DOIs cited; `274` net-new non-overlapping DOIs)**: retrieved and verified through **59 structured queries to the Consensus Academic Search API** (`https://api.consensus.app/v1/search`), updating `src/research-provenance.ts` to `citedPaperMatches: 433` (`159` across 11 retained Deep reports + `274` net-new DOIs). No uncurated OpenAlex fallback is used and no Consensus synthesis prose is copied.
- **Trusted non-DOI institutional & archival sources (`79` distinct URLs across 16 hosts)**:
  - **UNESCO World Heritage Centre Dossiers (`33` properties)**: Official property dossiers including Aksum (`15`), Lalibela (`18`), Carthage (`37`), Venice and its Lagoon (`394`), Delos (`530`), Syracuse (`1200`), Old Towns of Djenné (`116`), Kilwa Kisiwani & Songo Mnara (`144`), Tassili n'Ajjer (`179`), Leptis Magna (`183`), M'Zab Valley (`188`), Tipasa (`193`), Byblos (`295`), Tyre (`299`), Great Zimbabwe (`364`), Khami (`365`), Kairouan (`499`), and 16 additional North, West, East, and Southern African and Mediterranean heritage listings (`89`, `90`, `190`, `306`, `836`, `985`, `1021`, `1073`, `1099`, `1147`, `1226`, `1293`, `1336`, `1361`, `1475`, `1511`).
  - **The Metropolitan Museum of Art (`16` curatorial essays)**, **Pleiades Gazetteer (`14` verified ancient places)**, **UCT Zamani Project 3D surveys (`4` sites)**, plus mission, excavation, radiocarbon, and museum portals (`kerma.ch`, `hierakonpolis-online.org`, `sais.webspace.durham.ac.uk`, `giza.fas.harvard.edu`, `amarnaproject.com`, `banturivers.eu`, `sealinksproject.com`, `ostiaantica.cultura.gov.it`, `persee.fr`, `discovery.ucl.ac.uk`, `afrikamuseum.be`, `intchron.org`).

## Separation of `#atlas/africa/early` and `#atlas/africa`

Existing `data/world/africa-east.json`, `africa-north-west.json`, and `africa-south.json` remain unchanged (`africa-*`), preserving the `15,000 BCE` (`-14999`) cutoff enforced by `tests/africa-early.test.ts` on `#atlas/africa/early`.

## Remaining gaps

- Large subregions in Angola, South Sudan, inland Somalia, northern Chad outside Ennedi/Lake Chad, and rural areas between flagship mound/monument sites still have sparse radiocarbon coverage in English-indexed literature.
- Population totals are omitted except where primary archaeological surveys support site-level occupation estimates in Deep Dive prose; map dots remain locality anchors rather than ethnic or linguistic territory boundaries.
