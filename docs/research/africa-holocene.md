# Africa to 1900 CE plus Pleistocene Asia & Europe through 10,000 BCE

Research and access check: 20 November 2026.

## Overview

Across `data/world/`, six coordinated shards pair Consensus API academic literature with trusted UNESCO, museum, university and expedition sources to bring continental Africa cleanly through **1900 CE** and expand **Asia–Pacific** and **Europe** from the earliest hominin fossils down to **10,000 BCE**:

1. `data/world/holocene-africa-north-nile-sahara.json` (**56** records, **3** waves, **131** sources): Covers Maghreb & Cyrenaica (`Taforalt`, `Haua Fteah`, `Capsian`, `Kaf Taht el-Ghar`, `Carthage`, `Cirta/Tipasa`, `Volubilis`, `Leptis Magna`, `Kairouan`, `Sijilmasa & M'Zab`, `Fez`, `Marrakesh`, `Rabat & Tinmel`, `Ait-Ben-Haddou`, `Qalʿat Banī Ḥammād & Tlemcen`, `Hafsid Tunis & Ottoman Algiers`), Green Sahara & Oasis Networks (`Gobero`, `Nabta Playa`, `Gilf Kebir`, `Ennedi`, `Takarkori`, `Tassili n'Ajjer`, `Messak Settafet`, `Garama`, `Chinguetti/Ouadane/Oualata`, `Agadez`, `Ghadamès/Zuwīla`, `Touat/Gourara foggaras`, `Trans-Saharan Caravan Wave`), Nile Valley, Nubia, Funj, Darfur & Red Sea (`Jebel Sahaba`, `Faiyum`, `Merimde`, `Saïs`, `Hierakonpolis`, `Abydos`, `Giza`, `Amarna`, `Kerma`, `Barkal/Napata`, `Dangeil`, `Meroë`, `Old Dongola`, `Faras`, `Soba East`, `Sennar Funj Sultanate`, `Qasr Ibrim`, `Uri & Ain Farah Darfur`, `Suakin & ʿAydhāb`, `Fusṭāṭ & Cairo`), and Hijaz sanctuaries (`Mecca`, `Medina`).
