Interpretive limits

Calibrated and uncalibrated ages remain distinct. Separate hearths, burials and construction episodes do not establish continuous village residence. Typological dates remain identified as such. Coarse locality points may be several kilometres from the actual site; they are unsuitable for navigation. Genetic ancestry does not establish a language. Material sourcing does not identify a direct journey or trader. Population counts remain unknown unless explicitly sourced.

Important reconciliations include the single-sided canoe-canal construction constraint, newer Anzick dating, separate Last Supper Cave hearths, Aleutian mortuary-reservoir revisions, and the distinction between old feast debris and later mound construction. Earlier Bayshore Homes phase windows and Cooper’s Ferry Area B evidence remain intact.
