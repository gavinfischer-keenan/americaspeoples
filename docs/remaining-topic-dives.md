## What an empty map means

The linked [coverage audit](world-coverage-audit.html) separates sparse research, unresolved dates and missing locations. A record’s end does not become abandonment without evidence. The whole atlas retains shared context at the selected date; a focused movie intentionally shows only its subject. “In the shared history” reconnects its evidence to the master map.

## Decisions that affect the story

- Fort Ross’s sale concerned company improvements and supplies. It does not establish a consensual sale of Kashaya land.
- The 1911 convention has distinct sealing and sea-otter clauses. It is not described as a universal ban or proof of immediate recovery.
- Fortune’s Maui arrival is dated; erroneous log positions are not animated as an exact voyage.
- Atocha and the 1715/1733 fleets are storm losses. Covadonga is a wartime capture and is not counted as a wreck.
- Ana Angola’s Santa Marta arrival and later gold labor at Zaragoza are separate dated observations. The intervening route is not invented.
- Mission foundations, relocation and later buildings have different dates. The present São Paulo church is not projected into 1554; the large Trinidad church is not assigned to 1706.
- Monagrillo’s regional pottery range is earlier than its type-site occupation. Both records retain their own dates.
- Bordo Marcial’s mask is dated through an associated burial tooth. The pin is in Catamarca, Argentina.
- West Mexican metallurgy begins about **800 CE** in the cited publisher chronology. An erroneous **BCE** catalog rendering is rejected.
- Great Lakes copper peaks are models of sampled dates, not mining-output curves. Sicán experiments are not eyewitness records of ancient shifts.
- Regional anchors are explicitly labeled. They support regional orientation, not building-scale navigation.

## What still needs work

Detailed mine-to-port logistics, annual cargo and catch series, crew biographies, Indigenous whaling, local mission communities and many craft-production sites remain incomplete. The coverage report provides region-period priorities for subsequent additions. Finishing the movie queue does not mean the atlas has every town, journey or conflict.
