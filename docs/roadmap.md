# Research and guide expansions

The 25 Coming soon entries from v0.17 are implemented in v0.18 and v0.19. See [the completed list, links and source decisions](coming-soon-campaign.md). They add dedicated treatments to the existing Subjects, Places, Languages and Cultures lists; they do not exhaust those histories.

## Expand existing place guides

- Chaco Canyon: finer building groups, construction phases, roads and nearby communities.
- Machu Picchu: named structures, local sequence and connections within the wider Inka landscape.
- Cahokia: separate mound, neighborhood and surrounding-community histories.
- Mesa Verde: local villages and building phases beyond broad regional periods.
- Hovenweep: distinct settlements, structures and changing uses.
- Canyons of the Ancients: more public localities and place-specific chronology.

These six expansions are separate from the now-completed25-study queue. Public coordinates and source-supported dates are required; absence of a precise house date does not justify treating the whole landscape as empty.

## Continue filling geographic and temporal gaps

Use the [coverage matrix](world-coverage-audit.html) and [detailed 65-region audit](coverage-audit.html) to prioritize missing evidence without mistaking it for human absence. Continuing priorities include earlier Northwest Coast villages, Arctic local phases, Atlantic Brazilian landscapes, Central/South American communities after 1700, post-Lapita island histories, and later Holocene Australian and Aotearoa local sequences.

Further dedicated-place candidates include Tenochtitlan and the Basin of Mexico, Hohokam and Mogollon communities, Marajó/Santarém/Upper Xingu networks, and additional northern Andean and Pacific complexes. Existing guides remain available while more detailed phases are researched.

## Deepen the Australian studies

The wool and mining movies now cover actual towns, station buildings, transport, conflicts and imposed institutions through 1934. Continue to add individually documented station growth, mine phases, ports and shipping, with Indigenous participation, dispossession, resistance and forced relocation attached where the evidence establishes the link. More wool clippers and log-supported voyages would improve coverage. A schematic connection must not become a claimed daily ship track.

Keep company charters separate from built settlements, discoveries separate from operating mines, source alternatives separate from certain dates, and industrial change separate from an automatic claim of Indigenous consent. The modern post1934 resource boom remains outside the shared atlas. Only the existing Hawaiian political study extends to 1959.

## Research process

Use the completed Deep dossier and checked original sources before making another research request. Cumulative Consensus usage is 74/100, including status calls;26 remain under the agreed cap. New common historical evidence feeds the master map, focused subject material remains separate, and reciprocal evidence links preserve source-specific dates. The raw reports remain private.
