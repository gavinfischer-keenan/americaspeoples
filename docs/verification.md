# Current source verification

Version **0.28.0** contains **3,492 records: 3,419 shared observations and 73 focused topic records**. This voyage expansion adds 508 source-linked evidence records, revises 22 earlier records, and adds 87 dated journeys or phases. There are 81 guides, 83 contextual stories, 27 independent topic movies, 116 labeled terrain frames and 16 additional credited source-map assets. The 34 earlier guide photographs and plans remain available.

The [density review](coverage-15-review.md) ranks distinct dated localities and screens previous focused reviews. Its before/after comparison isolates the regional additions from simultaneous voyage work. Fiji, Tonga and Samoa are joined across the dateline; Potomac Creek remains outside the original rectangular Tidewater baseline while appearing in the guide. These are collection counts, not estimates of population or historical absence.

All place maps have zoom, keyboard controls, a whole-map reset and full-screen viewing. Configured whole-area frames include Chaco and the San Juan Basin, Machu Picchu’s sanctuary and Urubamba heartland, and the fifteen new regions. The original Chaco sheets retain the BLM survey legends and interpretation limits. Terrain images are 3,600 pixels wide, or 4,800 for Chaco and Machu Picchu. Elevation sampling and source accuracy are reported separately; output size does not establish surveyed detail.

The 161 dated itineraries and 105 year/month context entries share the atlas clock through 31 December 1934. Offscreen parties can be located without changing the date. Separate Endurance tracks show the ship, camps, Elephant Island party, James Caird, South Georgia crossing and Yelcho rescue. Untraced legs suppress an invented moving position. Approximate periods remain explicitly labelled; disputed polar claims do not become verified pole markers. The [voyage review](voyage-coverage-review.md) describes selected coverage and remaining route gaps.

The 57-stage release gate checks unit/data contracts, TypeScript, source-linked evidence, the build, SQLite/API behavior, workbooks, extracted self-host files and desktop/tablet browser scenarios. New scenarios check American and polar journey selection, dated endpoints, Cook's return under Gore, offscreen location without a date change, mobile layout and untraced Jamaica stranding context. Unit checks cover separate BANZARE field seasons and independent parties. Earlier scenarios continue to cover simultaneous polar parties, whole-area maps and all fifteen regional guides. Tooltip checks cover duplicate native SVG triggers, nested symbols, outside focus/pointer dismissal, Escape and stale redraw state. Existing date, continuity, trade, topic, map picking, accessibility and reduced-motion checks remain required.

The complete researched workbook uses shared strings to keep the expanded file within the existing 20 MB limit. Spreadsheet evidence and overlay sheets use the existing 5,000-proposal limit, which still applies to the combined import. Long record-scoped citation IDs are deterministically shortened; source text and claim links survive the full workbook round trip. The 5 MB compressed-file limit and formula, macro, external-link and ZIP safeguards remain enforced.

This campaign used no Consensus calls; **45 remain** in the authorized additional allowance. Public writing was independently authored from the cited sources, with private source-check excerpts excluded. Map assets retain their individual credits and reuse terms.

Final outcomes, independent review findings, public asset checks and rolling retention are recorded in [the v0.28.0 release receipt](https://github.com/gavinfischer-keenan/americaspeoples/blob/main/docs/releases/v0.28.0-verification.json). Only the current and immediately preceding release are retained for server rollback.
