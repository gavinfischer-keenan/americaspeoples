# Current source verification

Version **0.26.0** contains **2,710 records: 2,637 shared observations and 73 focused topic records**. It adds 68 records across the ten sparse areas documented in [the regional histories and coverage comparison](sparse-ten-histories.md). The atlas retains 66 guides, 68 contextual stories, 27 independent topic movies, 57 labeled terrain maps and 34 credited guide images or plans.

The map groups nearby places according to zoom and local crowding. Its verification checks that every eligible in-view point remains represented, that group members can be inspected, and that play/pause preserves the camera, date, selection and group membership. Directional animation follows explicit direction metadata; unknown direction and reduced-motion settings cannot acquire a moving directional trail. Graphic dimensions do not encode population or trade volume.

Continuity checks distinguish three situations: active source-supported habitation, earlier evidence retained between dated discoveries, and explicitly supported local departure. Routes retain their separate activity windows. The test cases include Orona's disjoint sample dates, continuing communities at Charenton and Koinawa, and Kiritimati's reported 1905–1912 occupation gap. Historical samples are not converted into continuously occupied towns.

The release gate runs unit and data validation, TypeScript checks, the production build, SQLite/API checks, spreadsheet round trips, the extracted self-host package and desktop/tablet browser scenarios. It also checks the older shared-world and topic movies, citations and licenses, terrain registration, map picking, date boundaries and reduced-motion behavior. Geographic coverage compares distinct locality IDs, clips modern named U.S. audit areas to their state outlines, and reconciles island-chain bins across the dateline. These are collection counts, not population estimates.

One Consensus Deep dossier supported the new research. Four calls were used including retrievals; **45 remain** in the authorized additional allowance. The full report stays private. New public historical writing links the underlying archaeological reports, community accounts and historical records. No third-party photographs or copied passages were added in this campaign.

The final test outcomes, independently reviewed changes, published asset checksums, live checks and rolling retention result are recorded in [the v0.26.0 release receipt](https://github.com/gavinfischer-keenan/americaspeoples/blob/main/docs/releases/v0.26.0-verification.json). Only the current and immediately preceding release are retained for rollback.
