Cahokia and the American Bottom

Cahokia stands in the American Bottom near present-day Collinsville, Illinois. Its monumental core belonged to a wider agricultural and settlement landscape. Earthwork dimensions and mound totals are architectural evidence, not direct resident counts.

Reading three parts of the centre

UNESCO places Cahokia's greatest prominence around 1050–1150 CE within a longer Mississippian history. Mounds served different purposes: some supported buildings, others contained burials or marked important places. The present protected property, surviving mounds, and the original metropolitan landscape are different inventories. Modern reconstruction, erosion, and excavation also affect what a visitor sees today. Source 1 ↗
