A closer reading

A landscape shaped by water knowledge

Channels, weirs and dams worked with wetlands to manage aquatic resources, including kooyang, or short-finned eels. At Muldoons, excavation distinguishes successive construction episodes. The system depended on relationships among features and water conditions, so an isolated wall makes more sense when read within the larger engineered setting.

Evidence: whc.unesco.org [1] ↗ · academia.edu [2] ↗

Budj Bim’s volcanic terrain supplied the ground on which that history unfolded. The eruption, the earliest dated deposits in a channel and later wall construction are different events. Their separate chronologies make human decisions visible within a much older landscape. Gunditjmara knowledge and continuing relationships to Country extend beyond what any one excavated date can describe.

Evidence: whc.unesco.org [1] ↗ · sciencedirect.com [2] ↗ · gunditjmirring.com [3] ↗

Country provides the setting; engineering has its own chronology

Budj Bim’s lava landscape gave later communities a particular terrain of rock and water. The eruption, the earliest dated channel infill and later wall building are different events. None should be used as a substitute date for the others. Source 1 ↗ Source 2 ↗

Reading a system rather than an isolated wall

Channels, weirs and dams worked together with wetlands to manage and harvest aquatic resources, including kooyang, short-finned eels. Excavation at Muldoons identifies successive local construction episodes; the whole landscape cannot be assigned one uniform building year. Source 1 ↗ Source 2 ↗

Three components, broad public references

The World Heritage property comprises a northern component around Tae Rak, central Kurtonitj and southern Tyrendarra. The public component coordinates orient the reader within a large cultural landscape. They do not identify individual channels, private places or excavation grids. Source 1 ↗

Residence, access and the companion community history

The Kurtonitj house and Lake Condah Mission add nineteenth-century histories to the engineering record. They have their own dates and meanings.
