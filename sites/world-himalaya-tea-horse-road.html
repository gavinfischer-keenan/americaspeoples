Himalaya & Tea Horse Road (Chama Gudao): Denisovans, Mountain Passes & Bhutan Dzongs

Far from an impenetrable barrier, the Himalaya and Hengduan gorges were traversed since Denisovans lived at Baishiya Karst Cave (>160 ka) and Nwya Devu blade-makers (40–30 ka), evolving into the dense Yunnan–Sichuan–Tibet Tea Horse Road and Trans-Himalayan salt networks.

Baishiya Karst, Nwya Devu, Pu'er–Lijiang–Shaxi–Dukezong Tea Road, Kangding–Chamdo–Lhasa, Upper Mustang sky caves, Rasuwa–Kodari passes, Gorkha, Bhutan Punakha–Trongsa *dzongs*, Sikkim Jelep La, and Leh–Baltit Karakoram corridors.

Pleistocene high-altitude Denisovans and 40 ka plateau settlers

Excavations at Baishiya Karst Cave (Xiahe, Gansu) recovered a ~160 ka Denisovan mandible, 48–32 ka Denisovan sedimentary DNA, and plateau-mammal subsistence, proving archaic humans mastered cold hypoxic plateaus long before Homo sapiens forged prismatic blades at 4,600-meter Nwya Devu. [5] [1] [6] [15]

Ancient genomes from Nepal's Upper Mustang sky caves and Ladakh confirm remarkable long-term high-altitude genetic stability coupled with trans-Himalayan cultural gene flow. [10] [16] [13]

Chama Gudao: The Tea Horse Road from Yunnan & Sichuan to Lhasa

To offset vitamin-poor barley-and-butter highland diets and provision Tang–Song–Ming cavalry with steppe horses, human porters and mule trains hauled compressed Pu'er and Ya'an brick tea through steep Shaxi, Lijiang, Dukezong, and Kangding gorges to Lhasa. [2] [7] [8] [12]

Nepal passes, Bhutan dzongs and India–Tibet corridors

Cutting north–south across the Main Himalayan Crest along the Kali Gandaki, Rasuwa–Kyirong, Jelep La, and Bhutan's Mangde Chhu trails controlled by Trongsa and Punakha dzongs, merchants swapped Tibetan rock salt and wool for Newar coins, Assam endi silk, and rice. [3] [9] [4] [17]

Karakoram–Ladakh caravans connecting South to Inner Asia

Further west across 5,500-meter Ladakh and Hunza saddles guarded by Leh Palace and Baltit's cribbed-juniper forts, yak caravans linked Kashmiri saffron and shawl looms directly with Khotan jade and Yarkand markets. [13] [14] [12]
