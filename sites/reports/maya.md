# Atlas of **Maya Urban Networks**

Maya urban complexes and inter-site networks developed through multiple construction, political, and demographic trajectories—not a single rise and collapse. The gazetteer below separates dated architecture, settlement observations, roads, political relationships, and unresolved evidence.

**Evidence notation:** **FT** = supplied full-text passage supports the entry; **A** = only an abstract or summary supports it; **M** = metadata only. These describe the material available here, not necessarily public access to the publication. **Unverified** means the supplied evidence does not establish the requested detail. No coordinates are reproduced because none of the supplied passages confirms numerical site coordinates.

## Research Gaps and Reuse

The largest limitation is **chronological assignment of extensive mapped landscapes**. Lidar records present topography; detailed construction and occupation histories require excavation, ground verification, and contextual dating [[74.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/archaeological-application-of-airborne-lidar-to-examine-inomata-triadan/26d674cefb395f1ea990299a98dc57fb/). Even systematic Puuc estimates face buried, perishable, and community-size-dependent detection problems [[55.4]](https://consensus.app/papers/lidar-survey-of-ancient-maya-settlement-in-the-puuc-region-ringle-seligson/e1c74f52fb815a04abab718bfbbbe92b/) [[55.5]](https://consensus.app/papers/lidar-survey-of-ancient-maya-settlement-in-the-puuc-region-ringle-seligson/e1c74f52fb815a04abab718bfbbbe92b/).

For reusable atlas records, preserve separate fields for **feature geometry**, **observation method**, **construction range**, **use range**, **abandonment evidence**, **dating material**, **calibration/model**, **political attribution**, and **source-access status**. Do not convert basin tiers, density clusters, survey areas, or dynastic influence into equivalent administrative boundaries: neighboring Usumacinta kingdoms exhibit contrasting settlement and agricultural organizations [[75.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/airborne-lidar-survey-densitybased-clustering-and-golden-scherer/a8be35fad035525d8ff92a05af9b5c11/).

Remaining priorities are exact building sequences for Calakmul Structure II, Caracol named complexes, Nakbé and El Mirador pyramids, Uxmal’s major buildings, and Chichén’s individual sacbeob. Supplied metadata alone cannot resolve the Great Platform excavation results, Plaza of the Seven Temples, or West Plaza sequences.

**Atlas takeaway:** publish independently dated places and features first; render uncertain phases and political connections as qualified layers. Capital abandonment, household persistence, migration, and later reuse must remain separate events.
