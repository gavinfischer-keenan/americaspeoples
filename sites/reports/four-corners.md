# Four Corners **Architecture and Settlement** Gazetteer

Four Corners architecture records overlapping settlement histories—not a single “cliff-dweller” population or a uniform Chacoan political territory. The gazetteer below separates archaeological regions, modern administrative units, settlement clusters, individual sites, and buildings, while preserving uncertainty in dates, functions, and connections.

**Reading conventions:** **FT** = supplied full-text excerpt; **A** = abstract-only evidence; **R** = review or secondary summary; **U** = unsupported by readable material supplied here. “Unknown” means unavailable in this evidence set, not necessarily unknown archaeologically. **Coordinates and resident population estimates are unknown for every entry below unless explicitly stated otherwise.** Room counts describe architecture, never inferred residents.

## 2. Site and Structure Gazetteer

**Spatial hierarchy matters.** Canyons of the Ancients National Monument is a modern protected area within the central Mesa Verde archaeological region; its project areas include several separate canyons and extend partly onto adjacent private land [[2.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/indigenous-architecture-and-rock-art-and-euroamerican-palonka/e4039a81dc415b51816ac752e850e33f/) [[2.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/indigenous-architecture-and-rock-art-and-euroamerican-palonka/e4039a81dc415b51816ac752e850e33f/). Chacoan “communities” likewise vary in spatial extent, population, and numbers of monumental buildings, and were not necessarily bounded social units [[5.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/chacoera-site-clustering-and-the-concept-of-communities-gilpin/a3e1e167129151a29e72417e045ac39d/) [[5.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/chacoera-site-clustering-and-the-concept-of-communities-gilpin/a3e1e167129151a29e72417e045ac39d/).

## 4. Site Narratives and Pages

### Lowry and Castle Rock

**Lowry’s page should separate masonry, kiva finishes, and modern conservation.** White-paint recipes changed from zinc/lead-bearing glossy layers to calcium-carbonate matte paint, documenting repainting rather than one static mural program [[18.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/lead-and-zinc-pigmented-mural-paint-discovering-ancient-rowe-desrochers/41d3e27f4562541aa22587dbfed5ad91/) [[18.4]](https://consensus.app/papers/lead-and-zinc-pigmented-mural-paint-discovering-ancient-rowe-desrochers/41d3e27f4562541aa22587dbfed5ad91/). Integrated digital and hand documentation produced HABS records for stabilization and archival use [[51.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/bridging-digital-and-analog-documentation-for-the-vlahos-macmillan/751a9596289550fe811b876c5d055736/) [[51.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/bridging-digital-and-analog-documentation-for-the-vlahos-macmillan/751a9596289550fe811b876c5d055736/).

**Castle Rock requires a settlement page and a separate community page.** Recent dating challenges assumptions that surrounding small settlements necessarily preceded the center [[17.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/ancestral-pueblo-settlement-structure-and-sacred-palonka-omeara/9aa48a11b7665c6c8785a666ee231233/). Mapping should connect architecture, rock art, shrines, and landforms without making every association contemporaneous [[17.4]](https://consensus.app/papers/ancestral-pueblo-settlement-structure-and-sacred-palonka-omeara/9aa48a11b7665c6c8785a666ee231233/). Older Basketmaker imagery occurs within much later Pueblo III architectural settings [[2.9]](https://consensus.app/papers/indigenous-architecture-and-rock-art-and-euroamerican-palonka/e4039a81dc415b51816ac752e850e33f/).

### Sand Canyon and Mesa Verde

**Sand Canyon’s architecture supports both residence and integration**, rather than a purely ceremonial or purely defensive designation [[12.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/planning-growth-and-functional-differentiation-at-a-bradley/862348f61fe75e318e16903303b5d9d6/). Public buildings across neighboring communities have also supported a proposed supracommunity planning model, which should remain an attributed interpretation [[52.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/creating-symmetry-the-cultural-landscape-in-the-sand-coffey/23fab0d7076b5f3ba5367c2dbc2844d3/).

**Mesa Verde’s cliff dwellings must remain embedded in their agricultural landscape.** Most late regional inhabitants lived in Great Sage Plain villages and dispersed communities, not within the modern park’s famous alcoves [[13.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/seeking-the-center-place-archaeology-and-ancient-varien/e7a7ad83bcf95a44b56607f743f1c0b7/). Check-dam yield modeling suggests substantial household surplus potential, but those modeled yields are not measured harvests [[53.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/check-dam-agriculture-on-the-mesa-verde-cuesta-reese/51b8171652445a08956531bf7bd3da9a/) [[53.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/check-dam-agriculture-on-the-mesa-verde-cuesta-reese/51b8171652445a08956531bf7bd3da9a/).

### Chaco and Northern Connections

**Pueblo Bonito needs a phased building biography.** Its chronology combines stratigraphy, wall relationships, ceramics, tree rings, radiocarbon, and archaeomagnetism, all complicated by repair and reused wood [[22.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/the-complex-history-of-pueblo-bonito-and-its-crown-wills/4fc6820def9b52f899b6e0e246289b82/) [[22.4]](https://consensus.app/papers/the-complex-history-of-pueblo-bonito-and-its-crown-wills/4fc6820def9b52f899b6e0e246289b82/). Canal evidence belongs in a separate landscape-infrastructure record: the proposed system was destroyed during paleochannel entrenchment approximately 1075–1150 CE [[54.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/the-archaeology-of-the-bonito-paleochannel-cycle-at-chaco-wills-williams/3675bf632e4e57059397fcdd0eefa986/) [[54.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/the-archaeology-of-the-bonito-paleochannel-cycle-at-chaco-wills-williams/3675bf632e4e57059397fcdd0eefa986/).

**Aztec, Salmon, and Chimney Rock are not interchangeable “outliers.”** Middle San Juan evidence supports both migration and emulation, varying among river valleys [[55.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/middle-san-juan-settlement-patterns-searching-for-chacoan-reed/e8d17553561c5c0fb0c333ce4ce2cca1/) [[55.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/middle-san-juan-settlement-patterns-searching-for-chacoan-reed/e8d17553561c5c0fb0c333ce4ce2cca1/). Chimney Rock comparisons distinguish the great house from nearby smaller settlements and show predominantly local ceramic procurement alongside wider exchange access [[32.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/chimney-rock-an-eleventh-century-chacoan-great-house-todd/f2f2d21aea015803886a05549b480f8e/).

### Canyon de Chelly and Continuities

Canyon del Muerto survey connected canyon and adjacent plateau archaeology and distinguished Ancestral Pueblo and Navajo settlement models [[56.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/the-canyon-del-muerto-survey-project-anasazi-and-navajo-fall-mcdonald/7a89c17cb0c7589c9e38db622ae5f175/) [[56.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/the-canyon-del-muerto-survey-project-anasazi-and-navajo-fall-mcdonald/7a89c17cb0c7589c9e38db622ae5f175/) [[56.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/the-canyon-del-muerto-survey-project-anasazi-and-navajo-fall-mcdonald/7a89c17cb0c7589c9e38db622ae5f175/). These regional findings cannot replace missing White House, Antelope House, or Mummy Cave building histories.

Homol’ovi evidence combines local production of ancestral Hopi ceramic forms with ceremonial-architecture comparisons [[10.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/winslow-orange-ware-and-the-ancestral-hopi-migration-lyons/fa2f93a239be555a84ac2ac1d4b3f0b6/) [[10.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/winslow-orange-ware-and-the-ancestral-hopi-migration-lyons/fa2f93a239be555a84ac2ac1d4b3f0b6/). Its Hopi connections involve movement in more than one direction, not simply northern refugees arriving after 1300 [[10.4]](https://consensus.app/papers/winslow-orange-ware-and-the-ancestral-hopi-migration-lyons/fa2f93a239be555a84ac2ac1d4b3f0b6/).

## 6. Gaps and Source Limits

The largest limitation is **building-level source access**, especially for Hovenweep, individual Mesa Verde dwellings, and Canyon de Chelly. Modern digital documentation is strong, but cannot independently supply missing occupation sequences, ancient populations, or functional identifications [[64.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/inventory-and-digital-documentation-for-uncovering-the-palonka-zych/cacdb9ad92595b20955f783712ed1f73/) [[51.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/bridging-digital-and-analog-documentation-for-the-vlahos-macmillan/751a9596289550fe811b876c5d055736/).
