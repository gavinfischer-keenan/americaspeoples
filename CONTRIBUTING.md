# Help improve Americas Peoples

Corrections, new sources, translations, accessible interfaces, and community knowledge are welcome. The project and its official website are noncommercial and free to access.

## Suggest historical evidence

Use the app's contribution editor and download your suggestion, or open a repository issue with the place, claim, source link, and proposed correction. The editor saves privately in your browser; it does not send or publish anything. A GitHub issue or pull request is public. Do not include private information or culturally restricted knowledge.

Include the historical date or range and how it was established, a source locator such as a page or section, location accuracy, disagreements, and what remains unknown. A paper's publication year is not an event date. Distinguish a community's earlier presence from colonial foundation, and documented abandonment from the end of surviving evidence. For violence, distinguish demonstrated injury or killing from uncertain cause and unknown perpetrators.

Write your own concise explanation. Link to papers and archives rather than uploading their PDFs or copying their abstracts or generated research reports. Identify third-party data and its exact license before proposing an import. AI-assisted suggestions need the same source checking as every other submission; disclose the assistance and verify each citation.

## Contribute code or content

Open a pull request describing the problem, resulting behavior, and relevant checks. Small fixes do not need a long proposal. For substantial additions, an issue helps coordinate source coverage and avoid duplicated work.

You keep ownership of your contribution. By intentionally submitting original code for inclusion, you offer it under the project's PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 software license. By intentionally submitting original educational content or curated descriptions for inclusion, you offer it under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Submit only work you have authority to license. Clearly mark separately licensed material; maintainers must verify compatibility before accepting it. No copyright assignment or exclusive commercial license is requested.

Existing source and contributor credits stay attached to updates. If correcting another person's contribution, explain the evidence and preserve the history. Publication requires editorial review; submission does not guarantee acceptance.
